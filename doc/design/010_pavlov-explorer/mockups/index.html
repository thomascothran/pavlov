■ Design step 1

EXPLORER UI CANDIDATES

Three clickable mock-ups. They share everything except how much of the program's shape is on screen. The data is real (generated from the model checker's states and transitions for three small programs) but the pages are static.

A · Trail

Local view only. The trace runs down the left with alternatives tucked under each step; the centre shows where you are and what can happen next.

Open

B · Columns

Local view plus one step of lookahead. One column per step shows what you took beside what you could have taken; each next choice previews what follows it.

Open

C · Map

Local view plus a map of the whole program. Your route and your next choices are lit up; click any point to go there.
